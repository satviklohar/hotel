Our Story

Where tradition meets wild flame

Since 2014 our kitchen has revolved around a single idea: great ingredients deserve honest fire. Every dish is touched by oak, cherry or hickory, sourced from farms within a hundred miles.

Our chefs trade in precision and instinct in equal measure, creating a menu that changes with the seasons and the smoke.
